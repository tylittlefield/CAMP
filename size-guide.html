How to Measure
  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, about 8" below your natural waist.
  • Sleeve: Measure from the center back of your neck, over your shoulder, and down to your wrist.
  • Inseam: Measure from your crotch to the bottom of your ankle.
