Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or sign up for our newsletter, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Payment information (processed securely, not stored by us)
  • Order history and preferences

We also collect non-personal data such as browser type, pages visited, and general location through standard server logs and analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Send newsletters if you've opted in
  • Improve our site and products
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
Cookies

We use localStorage and cookies to remember your cart, language preference, and session. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You may clear your browser's storage at any time.

Third Parties

We use Google Sheets to manage newsletter subscriptions. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Payment processing is handled by a PCI-compliant third-party provider.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe at any time.
