Sustainability
We believe the best gear is gear you never have to replace. Every fabric we choose is selected for performance, durability, and its impact on the places we love to be.
♻
Recycled Fibers
40% of our materials by weight use recycled or reclaimed fibers. We're targeting 70% by 2027.
🚲
No Per- and Poly-fluoroalkyl
All DWR treatments in our line are PFC-free. Water repellency without the chemistry that sticks around forever.
🌿
Natural Fibers
Where synthetic isn't necessary, we use wool and linen from farms with responsible land and animal stewardship standards.
What We Use
Our Materials
Merino Wool
Natural · Renewable
Fine-diameter merino regulates temperature in both heat and cold, resists odor naturally, and softens with every wash. We source from farms certified to the Responsible Wool Standard.
Used in: Peak Beanie, Base Layer Top
Recycled Polyester
Recycled · Durable
Made from post-consumer plastic bottles, recycled polyester cuts virgin plastic use without sacrificing strength or stretch. We use it across our fleece and shell fabrics wherever possible.
Used in: Summit Fleece, Camp Fleece, Harbor Crew Fleece, Wilderness Hoodie
3-Layer Shell
Waterproof · Breathable
A face fabric, waterproof-breathable membrane, and backer laminated together. Fully seam-sealed. PFC-free DWR on the face. Keeps water out and lets vapor escape when you're working hard.
Used in: Ridge Shell Jacket, Tide Jacket
Softshell
Stretch · Wind-Resistant
A 4-way stretch bonded fabric with a wind-resistant face. It moves the way you do, dries fast, and handles light rain. The go-to for aerobic activity in variable conditions.
Used in: Ascent Pant
Cotton-Modal Blend
Natural · Soft
Modal is a bio-based fiber derived from beechwood. It's exceptionally soft and holds color well. Blended with cotton, it makes a tee that gets better with age and washes without pilling.
Used in: Forest Tee, Wildlife Crew
Linen-Cotton
Natural · Breathable
Linen is one of the oldest performance fabrics on earth. It breathes better than cotton in the heat, gets softer as it ages, and requires far less water and pesticide to grow.
Used in: Shoreline Tee
PrimaLoft Gold
Insulation · Recycled
A high-performance synthetic insulation made from 55% post-consumer recycled content. Warm when wet, compressible, and long-lasting. We use it in place of down in any garment that may see moisture.
Used in: Explorer Vest
Ripstop Nylon
Durable · Packable
A grid-reinforced weave that resists tearing. Light and packable, with a PFC-free DWR finish for light weather protection. The right call when you want a jacket that weighs nothing.
Used in: Trail Jacket

Care
Make It Last

The most sustainable piece is the one that lasts. Washing less and washing cold dramatically extends the life of technical fabrics.

❄
Wash cold whenever possible. Cold water is gentler on DWR coatings and saves energy.
😵
Turn technical garments inside out before washing to protect face fabrics and prints.
💕
Tumble dry fleece and shells on low heat to reactivate DWR. Air-dry wool and linen items.
🚫
Avoid fabric softener on fleece and shells — it clogs fibers and kills moisture-wicking performance.
✂
Spot-clean shell jackets between trips. The less you machine wash, the longer the DWR lasts.
🔨
Minor repairs extend a garment's life dramatically. Contact us and we'll help when we can.
