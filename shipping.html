Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on items that are unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

How to Return
  • Email us at tyiosepa@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return
  • We'll send you a prepaid return label within 1–2 business days
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging if possible
  • Drop it off at any carrier location
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving the return
Exchanges

We offer size and color exchanges on eligible items. To exchange, follow the return process above and note which size or color you'd like instead. We'll ship the replacement as soon as we receive your return.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged item or the wrong product, contact us immediately and we'll make it right — at no cost to you.
