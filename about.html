Built for the wild.
CAMP makes gear and clothing for people who find themselves most alive when they're furthest from everything else.
Our Story
We started with one question.

Why does outdoor gear have to look like outdoor gear? We wanted clothing that could move from a ridgeline to a harbor, from a forest floor to a city sidewalk — without announcing itself.

CAMP was built around that idea. Every piece we make starts in the field, tested across the terrain that inspired its name: the peaks, the trails, the sea, the base camp where everything begins.

We keep collections small and deliberate. No seasonal churn, no trend chasing. Just well-made things that earn their place in your pack.
